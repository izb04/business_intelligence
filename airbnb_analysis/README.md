# Midwest Airbnb: Five Findings

- Hosts with at least 10 listings hold 37.15% of Columbus listings, 31.90% of Chicago listings, and 17.16% of Twin Cities listings, indicating that Columbus has the greatest listing share held by these hosts.
- The top 10% of listings earn 40.74% of Chicago estimated revenue, 36.08% of Columbus estimated revenue, and 40.27% of Twin Cities estimated revenue, showing substantial revenue concentration in all three cities.
- Columbus has a mostly-taken share of 0.1902697 among entire homes with October 2026 availability records, the lowest share of the three cities.
- Twin Cities reviews peak in August 2025 at 7,399, which is 2.372235 times the quietest month's 3,119 reviews, indicating seasonal demand variation.
- There are 456 listings with no availability rows: Chicago loses the most at 267, followed by Twin Cities at 120 and Columbus at 69, so availability joins exclude part of each market.
